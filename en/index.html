Munemo turns long text into a summary and next steps

Sales notes, meeting minutes, support tickets. Paste a long, unstructured block of text and Munemo condenses it into a few lines and suggests what to do next.

  • Paste long text as it is
  • Key points in a few lines
  • Concrete next actions suggested
  • Send to Slack, Jira or email in one click
  • Your data is never used for training
  • Translation built in: paste Vietnamese, get the summary in Japanese or English
